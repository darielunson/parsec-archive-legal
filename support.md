# Support for Duniverse Wiki

Need help, have feedback, or want to request your account and data be deleted? Contact us at **gothamforgeapps@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I search for a topic?**
Type any character, planet, or topic from Dune into the search bar on the Explore tab. If it already exists, it opens instantly. If nobody has searched it before, the app generates a fresh article in real time.

**How do I delete my account or data?**
Email gothamforgeapps@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

**Why do I see ads?**
Duniverse Wiki is free to use and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob. See our [Privacy Policy](https://darielunson.github.io/duniverse-wiki-legal/) for details on what data that involves.
